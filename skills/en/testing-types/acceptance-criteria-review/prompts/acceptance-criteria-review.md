# Acceptance Criteria Review Prompt
Audit supplied acceptance criteria. First list known inputs, missing inputs, assumptions, and risks; never invent business rules, fields, states, or pass decisions.

Output: scope; per-criterion verifiability review; ambiguity/gaps/conflicts; impact-ranked risks; 3–5 high-value questions; and a handoff to `requirements-analysis` or `test-case-writing`. With missing material, produce a labeled minimum draft.
