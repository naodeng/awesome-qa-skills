# UX Quality Perspective: Test-Case Review

## Inputs

- `stage: test-case-review`
- Existing test cases and linked UI/UX requirements, prototypes, flows, states, or design specifications.

## Applicability Check

First confirm that cases exist to review and at least one UI/UX evidence source is linked. If cases are absent or no experience-related material exists, return **Not applicable** with the reason, facts, gaps, and required material; do not fabricate omissions from a generic checklist.

## UX Questions

- Are cases traceable to existing task flows, information architecture, interaction states, consistency, responsive, or accessibility evidence?
- Do they omit any evidenced critical state, input method, device, or assistive-technology path?
- Are expected outcomes observable, reviewable, and free of unprovided UI assumptions?

## Output Contract

Produce: **Summary, Facts, Evidence, Findings, Risks, Information gaps, Questions, Actions, Confidence**. Findings identify only demonstrable coverage gaps, ambiguity, or unobservability.

## Evidence and Boundaries

- Do not guess screens, components, or breakpoints when prototypes are absent.
- Do not equate the existence of cases with execution, passing results, or release readiness.
