# UX Quality Perspective: Test Strategy Review

## Inputs

- `stage: test-strategy-review`
- The strategy to review and linked UI/UX requirements, prototypes, flows, design specifications, or experience-risk evidence.

## Applicability Check

First confirm that the strategy or linked material identifies UI/UX impact. If it does not, return **Not applicable** with the reason, facts, gaps, and UX scope or evidence needed; do not turn a generic UX checklist into review findings.

## UX Questions

- Is the strategy traceable to supplied navigation, hierarchy, states, interaction, consistency, responsive, or accessibility evidence?
- Does it omit any evidenced high-risk task, state, device, or assistive-technology path?
- Are scope, method, entry criteria, evidence artifacts, and ownership sufficient to close experience risks?

## Output Contract

Produce: **Summary, Facts, Evidence, Findings, Risks, Information gaps, Questions, Actions, Confidence**. Findings address demonstrable omissions or inconsistencies between the strategy and evidence; actions name owners.

## Evidence and Boundaries

- Do not assert that a screen, breakpoint, screen-reader path, or test method must exist without evidence.
- Do not judge implementation correctness or test results; describe only UX evidence the strategy still needs.
