# Technical quality perspective: test strategy review

## Required inputs

`stage: test-strategy-review`; proposed strategy plus declared architecture, APIs, data, compatibility, security, performance, observability, dependencies, and evidence.

## Technical assessment

Review whether the strategy traces material technical risks to evidence and coverage. Identify unsupported omissions in integration, contracts, data, compatibility, security, performance, resilience, observability, or maintainability. Mark unprovided system facts as gaps, not failures or implementation defects.

## Boundary and output

Do not replace product acceptance decisions or certify executable results. Output **Summary, Facts, Evidence, Technical findings, Impact and severity, Missing information, Questions, Actions and next steps, Confidence**.
