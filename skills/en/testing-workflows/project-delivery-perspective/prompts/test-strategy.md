# Project delivery perspective: test strategy

## Required inputs

`stage: test-strategy`; source-attributed target dates, available capacity, delivery dependencies, strategy milestones, accountable owners, and action status. Quality facts may be included only with their owning source for preservation, not assessment.

## Delivery work

Turn supplied delivery facts into strategy-planning input. Identify schedule/capacity trade-offs, dependency timing, milestone feasibility, ownership clarity, open actions, and escalation points only when the supporting source is supplied. State missing dates, capacity, dependency owner, milestone, or action status as gaps rather than assumptions.

## Source and quality boundary

For each project constraint and action, preserve the supplied source. Put defect, execution, test-result, evidence, quality, and release facts in **Preserved quality facts**, repeat their source and status without interpretation, and never rewrite or decide them. A request to alter a quality fact becomes a coordination action or question, never a changed fact.

## Output

Output **Summary, Project constraints, Action tracking, Preserved quality facts, Information gaps, Coordination questions, Next delivery actions, Confidence**. This output supplies delivery input to the test strategy; it is not a quality verdict or release decision.
