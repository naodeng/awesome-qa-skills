# Project delivery perspective: test report review

## Required inputs

`stage: test-report-review`; the report-review timing plus source-attributed meeting dates, capacity, dependencies, milestones, owners, action status, and any quality facts already recorded by their owning source.

## Delivery work

Track report-review delivery constraints and follow-up: meeting timing, available capacity, dependency blockers, milestone implications, accountable owners, action due dates, status, and escalation needs. Only record what the supplied sources establish. Treat missing source, owner, due date, dependency status, or capacity as an information gap.

## Source and quality boundary

Separate **Project constraints** and **Action tracking** from **Preserved quality facts**. Repeat quality facts exactly with their source and status for coordination only. Do not close or reprioritize defects, mark execution complete or passed, interpret evidence, issue a quality verdict, or authorize release; requests to do so remain questions/actions for the owning authority.

## Output

Output **Summary, Project constraints, Action tracking, Preserved quality facts, Information gaps, Coordination questions, Next delivery actions, Confidence**. This is delivery follow-up input, not a test-report quality verdict or release decision.
