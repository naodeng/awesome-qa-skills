# Skill Deprecation and Archival Guide

Deprecation may be proposed when a clearer Skill covers the capability, overlap cannot be reasonably merged, or maintenance cost lacks demonstrated value. A proposal supplies a replacement, retained boundary, migration guidance, affected documentation, and Eval/installation impact.

Deprecated does not mean delete: preserve directories and history, and show replacement paths in the Matrix, Catalog, Map, README, and Workflows. Archive only after the replacement is stable, historical installation needs are handled, and documentation remains. Never silently delete or use deprecation to hide an unverified Match conclusion.
